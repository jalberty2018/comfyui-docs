# 📚 LTX 2.x Resources

## LTX 2.5

### Official models, code, and research

- [LTX-2.5 model weights](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5) — gated
  repository containing the BF16, int8-convrot, and NVFP4 transformers,
  projected Gemma 4 encoders, distilled LoRA, VAEs, upscalers, and model patch.
- [LTX-2 source and pipelines](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2)
- [LTX-2.5 ComfyUI integration](https://github.com/Lightricks/ComfyUI-LTXVideo)
- [LTX-2 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03233)
- [LTX-2 Community License Agreement](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
- [LTX Studio playground](https://app.ltx.studio/ltx-2-playground/i2v)

### Workflows

- [Lightricks LTX-2.5 example workflows](https://github.com/Lightricks/ComfyUI-LTXVideo/tree/master/example_workflows/2.5) — single-stage, two-stage, IC-LoRA,
  video-to-video, and text-to-audio examples.
- [ComfyUI LTX-2.5 text-to-video template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_ltx2_5_t2v.json)
- [ComfyUI LTX-2.5 image-to-video template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_ltx2_5_i2v.json)
- [ComfyUI workflow-template repository](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates)

### Text encoders and prompt enhancement

- [Official projected Gemma 4 encoders](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5/tree/main/text_encoders) — BF16 and ComfyUI
  int8-convrot.
- [Gemma 4 prompt enhancer](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/gemma-4)
- [Community Heretic LTX-2.5 text encoders](https://huggingface.co/DeepNeuralNerd/Gemma-4-12B-it-uncensored-heretic-DeepNeuralNerd-LTX_2.5_ComfyUI) — BF16 and
  int8-convrot; experimental and not an official Lightricks release.
